This tutorial builds a to-do list that runs in your terminal: a list you move through, check off, add to and
delete from, that leaves itself in your scrollback when you quit and keeps its items in a file. Every step is
a complete program that compiles and runs, and each one extends the one before it. It starts from the counter
in the [Quick start](../../start/quick-start/).

Each step has tests you can run, and the programs are the files CI builds: steps 1 to 4 are in
[`examples/tutorial`](https://github.com/ayeshLK/module-termui/tree/main/examples/tutorial) and the finished
program is [`examples/todo-list`](https://github.com/ayeshLK/module-termui/tree/main/examples/todo-list).
The tutorial needs no account, key or network.
